Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 June 2026

This policy explains what data the MockMyCam website (mockmycam.kaarlmoroti.com) processes when you visit it. The short version: no accounts, no cookies set by us, no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising.

In plain terms: this is a static informational page. It doesn't ask you for anything, doesn't store anything on your device, and doesn't profile you. The only personal data involved is the technical information any web server unavoidably receives to deliver a page (mainly your IP address, in server logs).

Who is responsible

The controller for this website is Kaarl Moroti. For any privacy question or to exercise your rights below, contact: office@kaarlmoroti.com.

What data is processed, and why

Server log files

Like every website, when your browser requests a page the web server and the network in front of it automatically receive and briefly log technical data, which may include:

  • your IP address;
  • the date and time of the request;
  • the page or file requested and the HTTP status;
  • the referring page (if any) and your browser's user-agent string.

This is used only to operate and secure the site — delivering pages, preventing abuse and attacks, and diagnosing errors. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a secure, functioning website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). These logs are not used to identify you or build a profile, and are kept only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting, then deleted or anonymised.

Cookies & local storage

This site sets no cookies of its own and stores nothing in your browser's local or session storage. There is therefore no cookie banner, because there is nothing to consent to. The security/CDN layer described below may set a single strictly-necessary cookie; strictly-necessary cookies do not require consent under the ePrivacy rules.

Fonts

The site's fonts are self-hosted and served from this domain. They are not loaded from Google Fonts or any other third-party CDN, so your IP address is not shared with a font provider.

Hosting & content delivery

The site is hosted on a server operated by our hosting provider and is served through Cloudflare, Inc., a content-delivery and security network. To deliver pages and protect the site from attacks, these providers process connection data such as your IP address on our behalf (as processors), on the basis of our legitimate interest in availability and security (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Cloudflare may transfer data to servers outside the EU under appropriate safeguards. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Links to other services

This site links out to third-party services. When you follow one of these links you leave this site, and that service's own privacy policy applies:

  • GitHub — source code and app downloads (GitHub Releases). See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Ko-fi — optional, voluntary donations. If you choose to donate, the payment and any data you enter are handled entirely by Ko-fi on their platform; this site never sees or stores your payment details. See Ko-fi's privacy policy.

We have no control over, and take no responsibility for, the content or privacy practices of these third-party sites.

Your rights

Because we process so little, in practice there is usually nothing tied to your identity to act on. Still, under the GDPR you have the right to request access to your personal data, and its rectification, erasure, restriction or portability, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. To do so, email office@kaarlmoroti.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data-protection supervisory authority. In Romania this is the National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP) — dataprotection.ro.

Changes to this policy

If the site changes in a way that affects this policy, we'll update this page and the "last updated" date above.
